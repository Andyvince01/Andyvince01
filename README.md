### 👋 About me

<img align="right" src="https://github.com/Gapur/Gapur/blob/main/assets/coding.gif?raw=true" width="230"/>

I'm a **Ph.D. candidate in Computer Engineering** at the **University of Salerno** and an **AI Software Engineer** at **A.I. Tech**, working across the full lifecycle of computer vision systems — from research and model design to real-time deployment on edge hardware.

My work spans **zero-shot & foundation-model-based vision**, **agentic AI for video surveillance**, and efficient inference with **TensorRT, OpenVINO & ONNX Runtime** on heterogeneous hardware.

- 🔭 Currently building **agentic AI pipelines for intelligent video surveillance**
- 🌱 Exploring **vision-language models** and open-ended perception systems
- 📝 **8 peer-reviewed publications** (3 in Q1 journals) — 3 more under review
- 🎓 Selected among 200 of 632 applicants for **ICVSS 2026**
- 💬 Ask me about YOLO-based detectors, model optimization, or edge deployment
